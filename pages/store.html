Which gadget fits your project?

Raspberry Pi or M5Stack for a first build?

Choose the Raspberry Pi 5 for a home server that runs Linux applications. Include storage, power and cooling when comparing its cost with a kit. Choose the M5Stack Cardputer Adv for a pocket project that uses a built-in keyboard and screen, such as a timer or sensor readout.

R36S, Arcade1Up or Quest for a game room?

The R36S handheld suits portable play and a personal homebrew library. Choose the Arcade1Up Countercade for tabletop controls and its included games. Choose the Quest 3S for VR titles. Compare the games you actually want to play before paying for more storage or accessories.

Which USB adapter do I need for a removed SSD?

For a rectangular 2.5-inch SATA drive, see the StarTech SATA-to-USB cable. For a narrow M.2 stick, check its length and protocol against the Sabrent M.2 enclosure. A 3.5-inch desktop hard drive needs a powered adapter.
